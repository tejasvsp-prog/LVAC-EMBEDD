About the Summit

Where curiosity meets the cutting edge.

The LAVC AI Summit is a community-driven conference designed to demystify artificial intelligence for everyone — from first-time coders to seasoned engineers. Spend the day learning practical skills, hearing real-world case studies, and connecting with people building what comes next.

  • ✓
    Hands-on learningBuild, prompt, and ship real projects in guided workshops.
  • ✓
    Industry insightTalks from engineers and researchers at leading AI labs and startups.
  • ✓
    Career pathwaysMeet recruiters and explore internships, scholarships, and transfer programs.
  • ✓
    Open to allNo experience required. Students, educators, and community welcome.

Tracks

Six learning paths. One incredible day.

Choose your own adventure — every track is designed for both newcomers and experienced builders.

AI & Ethics

Fairness, transparency, and the human impact of automated systems — explored through real case studies.

Build with LLMs

Prompt engineering, RAG, agents, and shipping production-grade applications using modern AI APIs.

Data & ML Foundations

Build intuition for the math, the data, and the models — without needing a PhD to follow along.

AI in the Classroom

For educators: practical strategies, lesson plans, and policy frameworks for the AI era.

Creative AI

Generative art, music, and storytelling — where the algorithm becomes a collaborator.

Careers in AI

Internships, transfer pathways, and what it really takes to break in. Recruiter office hours included.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is this summit for?
Anyone curious about AI — students, faculty, professionals, and community members. No technical background required for most sessions.
How much does it cost?
The summit is free for LAVC students, faculty, and staff. Community tickets are also free while seats last.
Will sessions be recorded?
Keynotes and panels will be live-streamed and recorded. Workshops are in-person only to keep them hands-on.
Is parking available?
Yes — free event parking will be available in Lot D. Detailed instructions are sent with your confirmation email.
Will lunch be provided?
Yes, a complimentary lunch is included for all registered in-person attendees, with vegetarian and vegan options.
How do I become a sponsor or volunteer?
Email us at aisummit@lavc.edu — we'd love to hear from you.
