FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before the summit.

How much does it cost to attend?
The Lansing AI Summit is completely free and open to the community. We're committed to making this event accessible to anyone interested in the future of AI-powered work.
Is the event in-person, virtual, or both?
Both. The summit is a hybrid event — join us in person at Peckham, Inc. in Lansing, MI, or tune in virtually from anywhere. Choose your format when you register.
Do I need any AI experience to attend?
Not at all. The summit is built for the whole community — from curious newcomers to seasoned professionals. Every track is designed to meet you where you are.
Will lunch be provided?
Yes — in-person attendees enjoy a complimentary lunch as part of the Resource Fair, where you can connect with training programs, community organizations, and employers.
Can my organization become a partner or sponsor?
Absolutely. We welcome mission-aligned organizations committed to equitable workforce development. Reach out at mr.jamesbender@gmail.com to discuss partnership opportunities.
Will sessions be recorded?
Keynote and panel sessions will be recorded and shared with registered attendees after the event. Concurrent track sessions are in-person experiences designed for live engagement.
